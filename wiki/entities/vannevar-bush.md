# Vannevar Bush

_Originator of the [[memex]] (1945), the associative personal knowledge store that prefigures the
[[llm-wiki-pattern|LLM Wiki]]._

## Overview

Bush proposed the [[memex]] in 1945 — a personal, curated store of one's documents linked by
associative trails, where the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents
themselves. His vision was private and actively curated, closer to the LLM Wiki than to the public web
that actually emerged.

## Key facts

- Proposed the Memex in 1945.
- Saw the **connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves**.
- The gap he couldn't close — **who maintains the associative trails** — is precisely what the LLM now
  fills in the [[llm-wiki-pattern]].

## Relationships

- Originator of → [[memex]].
- Antecedent to → [[llm-wiki-pattern]] (cited by [[andrej-karpathy]]).
