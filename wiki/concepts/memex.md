# Memex

_Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between
documents — the spiritual ancestor of the [[llm-wiki-pattern|LLM Wiki]]._

## What it is

Proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in 1945, the Memex was an imagined device for storing one's books,
records, and communications and linking them by **associative trails** — the connections between
documents treated as being as valuable as the documents themselves. Bush's vision was private,
actively curated, and connection-centric — closer to the LLM Wiki than to what the web actually became.

## Why it matters

The Memex names the missing piece the LLM Wiki finally supplies. Bush could imagine the curated,
interlinked store but **couldn't solve who does the maintenance** — building and updating the
associative trails is relentless work. The LLM handles exactly that, making Bush's vision practical.

## How it relates to other ideas

- **Descendant:** [[llm-wiki-pattern]] — the modern, LLM-maintained realization.
- **Author:** [[vannevar-bush]].
- **Contrast:** the public, un-curated web — what actually emerged instead of the Memex.
